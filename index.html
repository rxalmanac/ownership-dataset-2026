Who owns pharma services?

The publicly disclosed ownership of 295 pharmaceutical services vendors (hub, specialty pharmacy, copay, market access, commercialization, data and RWE, and more) and 35 ownership events since 2024. Each vendor row links to its Rx Almanac profile, and each event row cites a public source.

How it was built

Every vendor with a published Rx Almanac profile on 2026-10-04. Each value comes from that public profile; ownership_type is Rx Almanac's classification of it. Events were checked against their cited source pages on October 3 and 4, 2026.

Full method, field definitions and known limits are in the README.
